Hi, I’m Mouad, a software engineer from Morocco focused on building reliable, scalable backend systems with Java and Spring Boot.

I enjoy turning real-world problems into thoughtful software, learning new technologies, and contributing to open source. I care about clean code, practical systems, and the details that make an application easier to maintain as it grows.

## What I’m working toward

My goal is simple: build things that solve real problems and keep improving my craft along the way. That means deepening my backend skills, staying curious about the wider technology landscape, and making time to give back through open source.

## Learning in public

I believe growth is more valuable when it is shared. Here, I write about what I’m learning, the projects I’m building, and the challenges that come with becoming a better engineer.

I’m not perfect, and I still have a lot to learn—but I put my work out there anyway. Sharing both the wins and the struggles is how we grow.
